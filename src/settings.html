Settings

Make Foxlite yours.

Appearance

Theme
Light, dark, or follow the system.
Accent colour
Tints buttons, highlights, and the address bar.
Default page zoom
Zoom level new tabs open at. Tweak any page with ⌘ + / −.
Reduce transparency
Use a plain opaque window instead of frosted glass — less GPU and compositing work, easier on battery. Applies on the next launch.
Show bookmarks bar
A row of your bookmarks under the toolbar (⇧⌘B).
Vertical tabs
Tabs in a sidebar on the left, with the address bar — instead of a strip across the top (⇧⌘S).
Switch tabs by recent use
⌃⇥ opens a switcher of your most recently used tabs: keep ⌃ held and tap ⇥ / ⇧⇥ to move, release to switch, Esc to cancel. Off: ⌃⇥ cycles tabs in order.
Home wallpaper
Pick a preset, paste an image URL, or upload a picture.

Search

Default search engine
Used for address-bar and home-page searches.
Address bar
Type a site or a search. Clicking the address bar (or the home page's search box) lists your recent pages; typing shows matching history, bookmarks and past searches — ↑/↓ to pick, ↵ to open. Addresses also complete inline (↵ accepts, ⌫ dismisses).

Bookmarks

Saved bookmarks
Manage the pages you've starred.

Performance

Memory in use
Measuring…
Block ads & trackers
Stops third-party ad networks, trackers and session recorders from loading at all — pages get lighter and faster. Applies on the next page load.
Block malware & phishing
Never connects to known malware and phishing hosts, and shows a warning before loading sites flagged as fraudulent (Safe Browsing).
Block pop-ups
Refuses windows a page tries to open on its own. Links and buttons you click still open in a new tab.
Hide cookie banners & consent pop-ups
Removes cookie-consent overlays and similar nag boxes. Turn off if a site misbehaves.
Sleep idle tabs
Unloads background tabs you haven't used in a while to free memory. They reload when you switch back; hover a tab to see what it's using.
On startup
Restored tabs load lazily — only the one you're looking at — so reopening 30 tabs costs the memory of one.

Privacy & security

Private tabs
Pages aren't saved to history and use a separate, temporary session (⇧⌘N).
Clear browsing history
Removes every page from your history. Can't be undone.
Clear cookies & site data
Signs you out everywhere and empties caches and site storage.

Developer

Web Inspector
Safari's inspector for any tab: ⌥⌘I, right-click ▸ Inspect Element, or the Develop menu. Applies to tabs opened after changing.
User agent
Sent to every site. Some sites serve better pages to a Safari/Chrome UA; leave on Default unless you need it.
